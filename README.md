## 🧑‍🎤 Custom Avatars from Your Own Footage

Custom avatars let you put a real person into your videos without booking studio time for every recording. You supply footage of the person, and the application builds an avatar from it.

When recording footage for this purpose, a few basics make a large difference:

- Use even, soft lighting on the face, ideally from the front.
- Keep the background plain and uncluttered.
- Record in a quiet room with minimal echo.
- Have the person speak naturally and look toward the camera.
- Avoid hats, strong patterns, and busy jewelry that distract from the face.

Good source footage produces a noticeably better avatar. Take an extra few minutes during recording rather than trying to fix problems later.

## 💡 Tips for Better Videos

Write your script for the ear, not the page. Short sentences, plain words, and one idea per paragraph keep viewers with you.

Front-load the important information. State the point in the first fifteen seconds rather than building up to it.

Keep individual videos short when you can. A focused three-minute video gets watched; a rambling twenty-minute one gets abandoned.

Watch your own finished video once as a viewer rather than as its creator. Anything that makes you want to skip ahead is something to cut.

Reuse your best setups. A proven avatar, voice, and script structure is an asset. Build on it rather than starting from scratch each time.
